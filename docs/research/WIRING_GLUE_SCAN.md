# Ad-hoc glue scan (companion of WIRING_COVERAGE.md): play mode vs ported functions (static read, 2026-10-21)

Scope: `apps/game/src/play/**` (qa/ skipped, `souls.rs`/`er_pack.rs`/`script.rs` only skimmed because they are opt-in or smoke harnesses), `crates/runtime/src/*.rs`, plus the seams that decide player feel and live just outside that scope (`apps/game/src/walk.rs`, `crates/sim/src/script_player.rs`, `crates/sim/src/sim/ai_exec.rs`). Evidence is by file:line at the time of the scan (several of these files are being edited concurrently; line numbers can drift by a few lines).

Column "called from play?": **yes** = the ported function is called by the play/runtime path; **no** = no caller outside its own file/tests (grep over `apps/` and `crates/`); **partial** = some entry points are used, the rule in this row is not. "No port" = no `Port of` function exists in the workspace for that rule (host rule is unavoidable but unverified). Severity = effect on player feel (high / med / low).

Two facts that explain many rows:
* The hero's body is owned by the host (`Command::HeroTransform`, `core.rs:752` overwrites the sim yaw/position each frame; the sim's `Goto*`/turn commands for the hero are dropped in `script_player.rs:564`). Anything the ported player states ask the hero to *do with his body* (turn, walk to a use point, play a clip of fixed length) is replaced by host glue.
* The presentation of hero and NPCs is event-driven by *damage moments* (`Hit`/`Whiff`/`Projectile`) and a few `PlayerEvent`s, not by the ported states' own `AiCommand::Action/AniStateAction/HoldInventoryStacks/TakeItem/Shoot/Cast/Parade/...` stream (`routines.rs:50` only maps roster NPCs; hero `Event::Anim` is dropped).
